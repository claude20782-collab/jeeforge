FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes — every mock, every solution, every feature. No paywalls, no locked analytics, no "premium" tiers. The source code is public on GitHub too.

What is the question level?

Genuine JEE Main difficulty, following the 2025–26 syllabus. The series ramps from hard JEE Main (Mocks 01–10) to very hard (Mocks 31–40) — multi-concept applications, tricky-but-fair patterns, no out-of-syllabus traps.

Are solutions available for every question?

Yes — and it is enforced technically: a mock physically cannot be published unless all 75 of its questions carry complete, verified solutions, answers, chapter/topic tags, difficulty and (for at least 20 questions) meaningful diagrams.

What devices are supported?

The CBT is designed mobile-first (Android phones), with a particularly polished tablet experience (palette side-sheet) and a full desktop layout. Slow connections are handled — answers save incrementally and resume after refresh.

How is cheating handled?

Reasonable, non-invasive protections: server-authoritative timers, one active attempt per mock, immutable submissions, server-side scoring, session anomaly logging, and rate limiting. No cameras, no surveillance.

Can I attempt a mock after its unlock date?

Yes — once unlocked, a paper (and its solutions) stays available forever. The schedule only controls when a mock first opens.

Platform status

Content engine: live. Mock 01 (75 questions, 22 diagram-based, complete solutions) is published and unlocked. Mocks 02–40 are being populated and validated on schedule — each publishes only after passing the strict 24-point publication validator.

Interactive app: the full application (auth, CBT, solutions, analytics, community) is deployed and continuously updated — source code: github.com/claude20782-collab/jeeforge. Production database: Supabase Postgres (region: Southeast Asia).

Data: student accounts, attempts and scores are stored server-side only; emails are never shown publicly, and leaderboard participation is opt-out.
